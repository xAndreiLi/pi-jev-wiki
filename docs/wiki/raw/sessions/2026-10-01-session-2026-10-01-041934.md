### In the first controlled A/B of the wiki (three tasks, three arms, nine runs, 2026-10-01) every arm passed every task and the wiki arm cost more on two of three, but its premium was dominated by wiki maintenance rather than consultation: 5-15 upkeep calls per run (capture, review, finalize at settle) against only 2-3 reads. Having the wiki installed costs agent work on every task whether or not the wiki is ever read, so any efficacy claim has to account for that overhead.

On that figure a 20% effect needs about 12 task-instances, though the estimate rests on three pairs and is optimistic.
