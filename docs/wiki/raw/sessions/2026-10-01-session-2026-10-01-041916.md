### In the first controlled A/B of the wiki (three tasks, three arms, 9 runs, 2026-10-01) every arm passed every task and the wiki arm cost more on two of three, but its premium was dominated by wiki maintenance rather than consultation: 5–15 upkeep calls per run (capture, review, finalize at settle) against only 2–3 reads. Having the wiki installed costs agent work on every task regardless of whether the wiki is ever read, so any efficacy claim has to account for that overhead unless the capture cadence is off.
